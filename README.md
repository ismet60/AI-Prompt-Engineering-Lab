A growing collection of hands-on labs in prompt design, Python, natural language processing, and business applications of AI. Each lab includes its own guide and the files needed to explore the topic.

---

## What This Collection Covers

- **Prompt engineering:** creating and refining prompts for practical business tasks.
- **Python and NLP:** processing text, recognizing entities, and analyzing sentiment.
- **AI applications:** exploring language models, research, customer support, and agents.
